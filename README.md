# GE2234 Social Networks for Media, Business, and Technology Applications

This is the practice website for GE2234 Social Networks for Media, Business, and Technology Applications.

Website: https://github.com/VinceeaWang/Ge2234

This course aims to introduce basic theoretical perspectives and research methods of social media and their applications for a wide range of media and communication applications (e.g., flows of news, entertainment, advertising, and public relations, P2P interactions, online communities, viral marketing, etc.). The students will be exposed to an interdisciplinary body of knowledge involving social sciences, information science and business studies. In addition, the students will carry out hands-on exercises, case studies, and group projects. At the end of the course, the students will gain not only intellectual knowledge, but also practical skills for scientific inquiry, critical thinking, problem solving, teamwork, and professional presentation to address social media issues. The knowledge and skills will also enable the students to help enhance human experience and life quality in the contemporary networked society.
